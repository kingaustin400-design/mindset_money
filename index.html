Business Ideas

You don't need to start big. Explore practical ideas, start with what you have, and grow one step at a time.

🛍️

01. Reselling

Find products people need, buy at a good price, and resell at a higher price.

Example

Buy an item for R80 and sell it for R120. Your R40 difference is before transport and other costs.

💻

02. Online Services

Use your phone or computer to help businesses with digital tasks.

Example

Create social media posts, edit short videos, or design simple promotional flyers for local businesses.

🥤

03. Food Sales

Sell snacks, cold drinks, or homemade food where there is local demand.

Example

If a snack costs R6 to prepare and sells for R10, the R4 difference is before other expenses.

🧹

04. Service Businesses

Earn by helping people with everyday tasks they need done.

Example

Offer car washing, gardening, or home cleaning. Start with suitable equipment and customers you can reach.
